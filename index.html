    A note for the night clerk

    Borrow a world. Make a way.

    Reach the glowing letter. Select a stamp to preview its effect, then click its dotted frame or drag the stamp into place. Each stamp exists in one place; moving it removes the previous effect.

    Ocean
    Raises the crate once you board. Inside the tide, F launches a Tide vault.
    Forest
    Grows a bridge you can jump through from below. Stand on a root and press F for a Root spring.
    Sky
    Reverses local gravity. F briefly releases you from it so you can drop and dash.

    Fold dash: Shift / X bursts horizontally. Landing, a world pulse, or finding a memory recharges it. Chain a vault into a dash to reach hidden memories. Hold jump for height; release early for a short hop.

    Glowing ✧ memories are optional. Checkpoint lanterns remember solid ground. Falling returns your letter safely; there are no lives or time limits.
