A note for the night clerk

A little world goes a long way.

Walk your paper courier to the glowing letter. Select or drag a stamp onto a dotted frame to borrow its world.

Ocean
Raises the wooden crate. Step onto it before raising the tide.
Forest
Grows a bridge. Move the same stamp to another frame when you reach solid ground.
Sky
Reverses gravity inside the blue frame. Walk under a ceiling, then leave the frame to fall normally.

Each stamp exists in one place. Moving or reclaiming it removes the old effect. There is no timer and no penalty for falling.

Keyboard: 1 2 3 select a stamp, Q / E choose a frame, Enter place, Backspace reclaim. A / D move, Space jump, R restart.
