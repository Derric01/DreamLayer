# POSTMARK

**Deliver places, not just letters.**

You are the night clerk at a post office for impossible destinations. Every postage stamp contains a miniature world. Borrow the sea to lift a crate, the forest to grow a bridge, and the sky to turn gravity upside down.

Each stamp can exist in only one place. Move it, and its old world disappears.

Four short letters take you from the forgotten shore to an address you might remember. There is no timer, no score to chase, and no penalty for a little tumble.

## Controls

Move with A / D or the arrow keys. Jump with Space / W / Up. Select a stamp to preview its effect, then click a dotted frame or drag the stamp into place. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 select stamps; Q / E choose a frame; Enter places; Backspace reclaims; Z undoes the last stamp move. R restarts the room; Escape pauses. Undo and pause buttons and touch movement controls are available on mobile. Sound is optional.
