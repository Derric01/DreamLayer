# POSTMARK

**Every letter finds a way.**

You are the night clerk at a post office for impossible destinations. Every stamp contains a miniature world. Borrow the sea to lift a crate, the forest to grow a path, and the sky to turn gravity upside down.

A stamp exists in one place. Move it, and its old world disappears.

Four short letters lead from a forgotten shore to an address you might remember. Fold into a dash. Vault with the tide. Spring from living roots. Let go of the sky. Find twelve optional memories on your way home.

No lives, timers, or penalty for a little tumble. Lanterns remember safe ground. The last letter waits with the light on.

## Controls

A / D or arrows move. Space / W / Up jump; hold for height. Shift / X fold dash. F activates the borrowed-world ability.

Select a stamp to preview it, then click its frame or drag it into place. 1 / 2 / 3 select; Q / E choose a frame; Enter places; Backspace reclaims; Z undoes a stamp move. M opens the map. R restarts the room; Escape pauses.

Touch movement, dash, ability, stamp, undo, and map buttons are available. Sound is optional. Use the fullscreen control for a larger view. Check Help for the movement combos.
